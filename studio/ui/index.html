Find something worth writing

      
Expands your topic using Google autocomplete — real queries people type, not guesses — then checks each one against the site's own sitemap. On a site with a couple of thousand posts, “have we already covered this?” is the question that matters, and writing the same post twice splits its own rankings.

      

        

          

        

          
          

        

          

        

          

        
      

    

    

    

  


  
  

    

      
Write the post

      
Written answer-engine-first: question headings, a standalone 40–60 word answer under each, a TL;DR and an FAQ set. Structure applied afterwards gives you an FAQ bolted on the end; writing to it gives you a post where every section is independently quotable.

Crawl the whole site

      
Duplicate titles, cannibalisation, thin pages, orphans and broken internal links only exist between pages — a per-post audit cannot see them. Throttled and resumable: stop it any time and re-run, and it picks up where it left off.

      

        

        

        

        

        
      

      
A limit or a filter makes it a partial crawl, and the orphan and inbound-link checks are skipped — on a partial crawl every link to an uncrawled page looks broken. Leave both blank for the full picture.

    

    

    

  


  
  

    

      
What are they covering that you aren't?

      
Diffs your page against up to three competitors. Returns a content-gap list rather than a score, because "you 62, them 71" is not something you can act on.
